Rooms

Choose a room and click the star field. Additional rooms must touch at matching openings. Scroll to explore.

Machines

Select a machine, then click a usable tile. Drag placed machines to move them.

Belts

Drag, or click to start and click to end. Start on/beside an endpoint to extend; end into another belt to merge. Click the same tile twice for one tile. Hover and press R to turn a tile or X to remove it. No crossings. Escape cancels.

Belt machines

Blue ports are inputs; gold ports are outputs. Splitter Mk. 1: 1 input / 3 outputs, shared evenly among connected outputs. Belt Balancer Mk. 1: 2 inputs / 2 outputs. Rotate to turn the port roles.

Machine input flow is capped at its recipe demand. Splitters redirect excess to outputs with remaining demand. Grey is below target, green equal, red over capacity; amber unknown.

At 200% zoom and above, each belt arrow becomes its flow rate in items/min. ? means unknown; hover for full details.
